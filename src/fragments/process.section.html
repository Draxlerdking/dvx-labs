Process

A clear path from idea to live.

01

Discover

Clarify the goal, audience, required features, constraints, and definition of success.

02

Define

Create the scope, technical direction, visual concept, timeline, and project milestones.

03

Develop

Design, build and test in focused iterations with regular previews and honest communication.

04

Deliver

Deploy, monitor, document and support the product after launch—so it keeps working as it grows.
